Stall PRD Architecture

Authentic Exhibition Display Approach

As documented in our PRD, jewellery is hung on racks behind the stall so it is visible at eye level, while framed art and brass handicrafts are arranged on presentation tables with clear price cards. Dual-side lighting keeps the metalwork and natural pigments bright and vibrant.

Three Operational Roles (PRD Section 6)

• Role 1: Customer Engagement — Welcoming visitors, explaining the craft technique, and sharing heritage stories.

• Role 2: Sales & Payments — Handling enquiries, managing UPI payments, and careful packing.

• Role 3: Display & Inventory — Maintaining eye-level racks, presentation tables, and inventory counts.

Our Story & Craft Origins

Rooted in Rural Odisha

Based in Vill-Adipura, Dhenkanal, Ipsita Producer Group unites traditional women artisans dedicated to preserving Odisha's rich tribal art and lost-wax metal craft.

Working under the Odisha Government's Subhadra Shakti initiative and registered under Udyam (UDYAM-OD-09-0050793), the enterprise bridges indigenous artisans with cultural exhibitions like the AKAMA College Trade Fair, empowering artisans through fair trade and direct connection.
